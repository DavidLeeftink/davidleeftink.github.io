I am a PhD candidate at the Donders Institute for Brain, Cognition and Behavior (Radboud University), supervised by [Dr. Max Hinne](https://www.ru.nl/personen/hinne-m) and [Prof. Marcel van Gerven](https://www.ru.nl/personen/gerven-m-van).

My research sits at the intersection of *(probabilistic) machine learning*, *dynamical systems**, and *control theory*; this work frames my approach to *reinforcement learning*. A central question throughout my work is: how do we make **trustworthy** and **robust** decisions under model **uncertainty**?

* **Theory:** I bridge the gap between optimal control theory and deep reinforcement learning. By rooting modern algorithms in formal notions of **safety**, **robustness**, and **stability**, I aim to make AI viable for high-stakes environments.

* **Practice:** I develop decision-making algorithms for complex real-world systems. My work has been deployed to optimize [semiconductor manufacturing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23141) and to adapt stimulation patterns in [neural implants](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/1741-2552/adeae9/meta) to restore visual percepts for the blind.

My aim is to root modern reinforcement learning algorithms in the notions of <span style="color: var(--global-theme-color)">safety</span>, <span style="color: var(--global-theme-color)">robustness</span>, and <span style="color: var(--global-theme-color)">stability</span>. These are **essential** characteristics of intelligent decision-making in **high-stakes** applications such as healthcare, energy systems, and robotics.
